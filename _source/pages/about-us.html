Hello. We're Humblebee.

A digital studio working with ambitious international companies.

We're a diverse team. We love what we do and have fun doing it.

Our Values

Diversity

We believe diversity means different opinions leading to a better atmosphere and fostering creativity.

Equality

Everyone's opinion is of equal importance at Humblebee and everyone has the opportunity to develop.

Respect

We work in collaborative teams and we respect our fellow colleagues and partners at all times.

Co-creation

Co-creation is the key to success. We put the right experts in a room and great things happen.

Speed

Speed to get services to market is a differentiator for smart companies and brings competitive advantage.

No Bullsh*t

We always aim to find and build what's needed, not always what's asked for. We always give our customers honest straight talk.

Hive - our program for international students

Hive is our student lab. We assemble a team of international students to tackle a challenge. This team has 6 months to design and develop a digital service to address a real-world challenge. Students are given mentors in respective fields and are based alongside Humblebee at our lovely Gothenburg, Sweden HQ.
