Security Trends in 2025

Humblebee understand the security services landscape. We can help you stay ahead of the curve.

Technology

The security industry is rapidly adopting new technologies, such as AI, machine learning, and cloud-based solutions, to enhance security measures and improve efficiency. This includes AI-powered video analytics & facial recognition.

Integrated solutions

Customers are increasingly seeking integrated solutions that combine physical security with electronic systems, remote monitoring, and data analytics. This trend is driven by the need for more comprehensive and pro-active security measures.

Cybersecurity

With the increasing frequency and sophistication of cyberattacks, cybersecurity and data protection are becoming paramount concerns. This includes the adoption of zero-trust architecture and the development of quantum encryption techniques.

AI

AI and machine learning are being used to enhance threat detection, automate security tasks, and improve risk assessment. AI-powered systems can analyse vast amounts of data to identify patterns and anomalies, enabling more proactive and intelligent security measures.

Cloud based systems

Cloud-based systems offer scalability, flexibility, and cost-effectiveness, driving their adoption in the security industry. Cloud-based solutions also enable real-time updates and remote access, enhancing security management and response capabilities.

Sustainability

The security industry is increasingly focused on minimising its environmental impact and promoting sustainable practices. This includes adopting energy-efficient technologies, reducing waste, and promoting responsible sourcing of materials.
